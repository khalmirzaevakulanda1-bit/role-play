Introduction

Role-playing games provide opportunities for students to connect classical language knowledge with communication, translation, historical interpretation and collaboration.

This textbook includes thirty learning activities organized into five modules. Every lesson contains learning objectives, a Latin dialogue, vocabulary, role assignments, practical exercises, control questions and assessment criteria.

Learning Objectives

  1. Develop confidence in reading and using Latin expressions.
  2. Understand vocabulary and grammatical patterns in context.
  3. Practice translation and intercultural communication.
  4. Develop creativity through structured role-playing.
  5. Evaluate linguistic accuracy and collaborative performance.

Methodology

Students should read the model dialogue, analyze the vocabulary, select roles, prepare a performance, complete the practical activities and reflect on the results.

Conclusion

Role-playing activities encourage active participation and make classical language learning more engaging. Students develop linguistic knowledge together with teamwork, creativity and intercultural awareness.

The activities may be adapted to different university groups and classroom conditions.
